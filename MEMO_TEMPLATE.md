# Targeted Memo Template

A one-page memo is the cold-outreach tool. It is not a CV, not a cover letter, not a research summary. It is a *targeted application of your work to something the recipient cares about*. That specificity is what gets a reply.

This file contains the template plus three worked examples for specific UK/EU firms and institutions, calibrated to what each has recently published or worked on.

---

## How a targeted memo works

The structure is identical regardless of recipient. Three paragraphs, plus a closing.

**Paragraph 1 — Address something specific they have done.** Not a generic compliment. A specific paper, case, or publication, named. This proves you have read their work, which is the single highest-value signal in a cold email.

**Paragraph 2 — Connect your work to theirs.** One sentence that shows the bridge. Not a dump of your CV. A specific point of intersection.

**Paragraph 3 — Make a small ask.** Not "give me a job." A 15-minute call, a comment on the white paper, an introduction. Small asks have high reply rates.

**Closing — Two attachments and contact details.** The Executive Brief PDF and a link to the landing page. That is all.

The whole thing should fit on one screen. If it scrolls, it is too long.

## Five rules

**1.  Read their recent work before writing the memo.** If you cannot name a specific piece, you are not ready to send. Generic outreach has reply rates near zero. Specific outreach has reply rates above 20%.

**2.  Do not attach the full paper to the cold email.** Attach the one-page Brief. The paper goes via the landing-page link, where the recipient self-selects into reading it. Asking a partner to open a 6-page PDF cold is a friction point.

**3.  Do not ask for a job.** Ask for a reaction, a 15-minute call, or a comment. The job conversation only happens if the substantive conversation goes well.

**4.  Send to a specific named person, not a generic inbox.** "info@" addresses are read by office managers. Names are read by the named person.

**5.  One follow-up after 10 working days. No more.** A single short follow-up doubles your reply rate. Three follow-ups halve it.

## Target list (build before you arrive at QUB)

Identify five recipients before you start the LLM. Write five memos. Send them in week one of term. The objective is not five offers — it is one substantive conversation. Five attempts is the right denominator.

Suggested categories (you fill in the named individuals from each):

- **One UK IP boutique** — Bristows, Powell Gilbert, or HGF
- **One Irish firm** — A&L Goodbody, Matheson, or William Fry
- **One UK academic** — at QUB, KCL, Oxford CCLS, or Bournemouth (they accept early-stage research collaborations)
- **One IP office** — UK IPO policy team, EUIPO Observatory, or EPO Boards of Appeal research
- **One legal-tech / commercial target** — Lexis Nexis Innovation team, Thomson Reuters legal AI division, or a smaller firm that licences tools

The framework above works for all five. You change one paragraph (the specific reference to their work) and the closing ask. Everything else stays.
